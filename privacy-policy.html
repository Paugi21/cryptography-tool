Cryptography Tool – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026
Applies to: Cryptography Tool for Windows (Microsoft Store) and Cryptography Tool for Android

Summary

Cryptography Tool does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. All encryption and decryption happens locally on your device. The app has no user accounts and never connects to the internet.

Information we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, telemetry or crash reporting, and does not send any data to us or to third parties. Because nothing is collected, nothing is disclosed to any party.

Your files, passwords and keyfiles

  • The app only reads files and folders that you choose (by drag-and-drop or a file dialog) and only writes output to locations you choose.
  • Passwords, passphrases and keyfile contents are held in memory only for the duration of an operation and are never saved to disk or transmitted.
  • The optional “Delete files” setting deletes the original files only when you enable it for an operation.

Windows permissions

On Windows the app runs as a standard desktop application (the runFullTrust capability required for all desktop apps in the Microsoft Store). It does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or any other capability.

Your controls

Since no data is collected, there is no data to access, export or delete. You can remove the app at any time from Settings › Apps (Windows) — uninstalling removes the app completely. Your own encrypted files remain where you saved them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
